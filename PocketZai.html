What is the OWNER_KEY?

Short answer: you probably don't need it. Your relay keeps your z.ai sign-in so you stay signed in — it locks itself to the first account that signs in on it. No key, nothing to type.

The OWNER_KEY is an optional extra lock. If your worker file has one set, the relay hands its saved sign-in only to apps that type the same key in the box above — once. Anyone else holding your relay address gets a plain guest proxy, never your account.

How to set one:

  1. Tap Copy worker code in the share card below (or open your worker in Cloudflare).
  2. The OWNER_KEY line is at the very top of the worker file, clearly marked.
  3. Put your secret between the quotes: const OWNER_KEY = "my-secret-key";
  4. Save & Deploy in Cloudflare.
  5. Type the same key in the box above and tap Save. This phone stays signed in.

People you share this app with deploy their own worker — they can leave the key empty (easiest, that's the default) or set their own the exact same way.
